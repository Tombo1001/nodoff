Only monitors with "Publish to MQTT" turned on appear in Home Assistant. A monitor that doesn't answer may have DDC/CI turned off in its own on-screen menu.

nodoff
What it doesLets MQTT, and so Home Assistant, set each monitor's brightness and put it to sleep or wake it, over DDC/CI. Pair it with a presence sensor and your screens nod off when you leave the desk.
